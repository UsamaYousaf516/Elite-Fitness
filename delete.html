Delete Account – Elite Fitness

This page explains how users of the Elite Fitness mobile app can permanently delete their account and all associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly inside the app:

  1. Open the Elite Fitness app.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete My Account.
  3. Confirm your deletion request.

Once confirmed, your account data will be permanently deleted from our systems within 7 days.

Alternatively, You Can Email Us

If you can’t access your account, email support@elitefitness.com with the subject “Delete My Account” and include your registered email address.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Name and contact information
  • Age, height, weight, and workout preferences
  • Workout progress and history
  • Firebase authentication record

Data That May Be Retained

Minimal technical logs may be retained for up to 30 days for security or legal compliance before full deletion.

Contact

For any account or data-related concerns, reach us at support@elitefitness.com.

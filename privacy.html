Privacy Policy — Elite Fitness

Last updated: October 27, 2025

Elite Fitness (“we”, “our”, “us”) built the Elite Fitness mobile app. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application and related services. By using the app you agree to the collection and use of information as described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of data to provide the app’s core functionality for gym members:

  • Account & Authentication: Email address, user ID (Firebase Auth).
  • Profile & Personal Info: Name, age, gender (optional), height, weight.
  • Fitness Data: Workout preferences, plans, progress and history stored in your account.
  • Device & Usage: Basic device identifiers and anonymous analytics (if enabled) to help us improve the app.

2. How We Collect Data

Data is provided by you when you sign up, complete profile fields, or use features in the app. We also collect certain technical information automatically (device type, OS version) needed to operate and debug the app.

3. Why We Use Your Data

  • Account management: to authenticate and manage your membership access.
  • App functionality: to store and show workout plans, preferences, and progress.
  • Support & compliance: to respond to support requests, abuse reports, or legal requests.
  • Analytics (optional): to measure app performance and improve features.

4. Where We Store Data & Third Parties

We use Firebase (Google Cloud) to store and process your data. Firebase services we use may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore / Realtime Database
  • Cloud Storage (for images)
  • Crashlytics / Analytics (if enabled)

Firebase uses HTTPS/TLS for data in transit and encrypts data at rest. For details see Firebase docs.

5. Is Data Shared with Third Parties?

No. We do not sell or share personal user data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. We may share information with service providers who help us operate the app (for example, Firebase). These providers are only allowed to use your data to provide services to us.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

The mobile app does not use browser cookies. We may store small pieces of data locally on the device (cached settings) for app performance. Sensitive personal data is saved on our Firebase backend, not in plain local files.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

When you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data from our primary databases within 7 days. Minimal technical logs (for security or compliance) may be retained for up to 30 days before permanent deletion.

To request deletion, use the in-app Settings → Delete My Account feature or visit our deletion page: Delete Account.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data — including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) and restricted access on our backend. However, no system is 100% secure; if you suspect a security issue, contact us immediately.

9. Children

Our app is intended for gym members and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us to request removal.

10. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

GDPR (EU residents): You may request access, correction, portability or deletion of your personal data. CCPA (California residents): Residents may request disclosure of categories of personal data collected and request deletion.

To exercise any rights, contact us at support@elitefitness.com. We'll respond as required by applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated via the app or email when appropriate.
